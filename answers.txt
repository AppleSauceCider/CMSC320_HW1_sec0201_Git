
Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git is helpful when collaborating with others. It is also very useful for version control, it keeps a version of your code in the cloud so changes you make locally are not permanent.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science can be applied in a field like sales. When selling cars, it is important to know who is likely to buy a car and what kind of cars to produce. Data science is helpful to get these insights and apply them to business operations. 

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merge combines two branches by creating a new merge commit that ties their histories together. Rebase moves you feature branch commits to the tip of the target branch, rewriting history into a straight path.
